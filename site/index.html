FAQ

Practical details about offline audio, privacy, supported files, and installing Papercut on each platform.

Library and Privacy

Which document formats are supported?

Papercut imports HTML, EPUB, PDF, TXT, and Markdown files. PDFs with embedded text are searchable immediately; image-only and hybrid pages can be recognized on demand in English or Arabic with offline OCR. Supported Android devices can also scan pages or import photos into a PDF.

Does Papercut use AI for anything besides audiobooks?

Yes. Papercut also uses bundled, on-device OCR to recognize English or Arabic text in image-only and hybrid PDF pages. OCR and audiobook generation do not upload your documents to a cloud service.

To search by meaning, download the optional search model, enable Semantic Search (Experimental), and build the index on your device. You can then search offline.

When does Papercut need internet access?

Downloads and external links use the internet. Direct transfers use the local network when you choose to send books or audio to another device.

Voices and Languages

Which interface languages are available?

Papercut is available in English, Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Which audiobook languages are available?

Kokoro supports English and offers experimental voices for Mandarin Chinese, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese. Supertonic offers experimental English and Arabic voices.

Why does English audiobook generation use Kokoro?

Kokoro is the default English model. Once downloaded, it generates speech locally without an API key or per-character fees.

How does Arabic audiobook support work?

Piper Kareem supports Arabic. Supertonic is an experimental alternative. SILMA requires an optional runtime on Linux x64.

Audio Rights and Sharing

Who owns generated audio?

Papercut does not claim ownership of generated audio. You are responsible for rights in the source text, any reference voice or recording used, and how exported audio is shared. Papercut saves audiobook output locally and does not upload it to a service.

Can I move or share generated audiobooks?

Yes. Saved audiobooks can be exported as Papercut audiobook bundles, then shared, transferred to another device, backed up, or imported back into Papercut later. If you share, publish, sell, or broadcast generated audio, label it as AI-generated and do not use it to impersonate or mislead.

Can I clone or reference any voice?

No. Reference-voice features should only be used with voices and recordings you have rights or permission to use.
