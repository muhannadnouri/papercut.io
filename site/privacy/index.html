Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the Papercut Offline app and the website at trypapercut.app, operated by the maintainers of Papercut. Your library stays on your device by default. The website receives the information you choose to send through its contact form.

What the app stores on your device

Papercut keeps imported and pasted documents, recognized text from scans, search indexes, folders, bookmarks, reading and playback positions, settings, and saved audiobooks on your device. On supported devices, Papercut uses the camera when you choose to scan a page. Reading, search, text recognition, and audiobook generation run on your device after any required model has been downloaded.

The app has no Papercut account, hosted library, advertising, or built-in usage tracking. Papercut does not send your library, search queries, or generated audio to its developer or to a cloud service for processing. We do not sell personal information or use your library to train models.

Downloads, sharing, and backups

  • Downloads. App releases and optional voice or other model downloads may contact GitHub or, for supported optional desktop features, Hugging Face. The download provider receives information needed to deliver the request, such as your IP address and the requested file. Your library is not included in these requests. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Hugging Face Privacy Policy.
  • Local transfer and exports. When you start a nearby transfer, Papercut uses your local network to pair devices and sends the content you select directly to the receiving device over an encrypted connection. Exported library files are unencrypted. When you export or share a document or audiobook, the destination you choose receives a copy. Keep export files somewhere you trust.
  • System services. Your operating system, app store, file provider or backup service may handle information under its own settings and privacy terms. For example, the system may back up Papercut data to iCloud if your settings allow it. External sites you open from the app or website have their own privacy practices.

Website and support

Netlify hosts the website and processes submissions sent through Netlify Forms. A submission includes your email address, topic, subject and message, plus any optional details you enter, such as your name or a requested language or voice. We use this information to respond, resolve problems and understand requests for improvements. Avoid including private documents, passwords or sensitive information.

Netlify may process technical request information such as IP addresses for website delivery, security and spam prevention. Website or support information may be processed in countries other than your own. Service providers that process personal information for us must protect it to a standard comparable to this policy. We may disclose information when required by law. See Netlify’s Privacy Statement and its form submission documentation.

Retention, deletion, and choices

App data stays on the device until you remove it. You can delete individual items in Papercut or use Settings → Library & Data → Review Data to Erase → Erase Everything to clear your library, settings and downloaded models from the app. Manage originals, exports, copies on other devices and operating system backups separately.

You can skip optional downloads and transfers, or revoke camera and local-network permissions in your device settings. You can also contact us to withdraw consent to any ongoing use of information you submitted, subject to any information we need to keep by law.

We keep support submissions and related correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, then delete or de-identify the information unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

Privacy questions and requests

Use the website contact form and select Other to contact the maintainers of Papercut about privacy. You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise a concern. Please provide only the details needed to identify the request. We may ask you to verify that it relates to your information.

We will post policy changes here and update the date above. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it before using your information for the new purpose.
