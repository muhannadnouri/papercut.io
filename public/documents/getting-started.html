Getting Started with Papercut

Papercut helps you search, read, and listen to your personal document library offline. Import HTML or EPUB files, find passages across your collection, and generate saved audiobooks from documents you want to hear later.

Start with the three tabs

  • Search: search bundled and imported documents.
  • Library: browse, import, organize, and open documents.
  • Audiobooks: manage saved audio and imported audiobook bundles.

Import your first document

Open the Library tab, choose Import, then pick an HTML or EPUB file from your device. Imported documents are stored locally and can be searched, read, moved into folders, or deleted from this device later.

Read and listen

Open any document from Search or Library to read it. If audio is supported on your device, use the audio controls to generate speech locally. You can save a full audiobook, pause and resume longer saves, then export the finished bundle for backup or transfer.

Privacy basics

Papercut is designed for offline-first use. Your imported documents and generated audio stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.
