# Papercut v1.11.1

## Reading on iPhone and iPad

- The Aa menu stays inside the safe area when you open it after scrolling or rotate the phone. On shorter screens, scroll inside the menu to reach Reset.
- Opening a search result near the beginning of a book keeps the toolbar in view when the full match fits on screen. Results that open a chapter or section also keep its opening in view.
- In a reflowable book with saved audio, opening playback options keeps the reader toolbar and audiobook count clear of the status bar.
- Play from here skips text covered by the status bar. Tapping Play in the toolbar closes playback options before choosing the passage.
