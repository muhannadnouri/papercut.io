Papercut helps you read, search and listen to documents on your device.

• Import HTML, EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown files.
• Find words and passages across your library, or enable optional conceptual search.
• Read with bookmarks, adjustable fonts and reading preferences.
• Recognize English and Arabic text in scanned PDFs using local OCR.
• Scan pages with your camera and review them before importing.
• Generate audiobooks locally, listen in the background, and import or export saved audio.
• Transfer library content between your devices over your local network.

Reading, keyword search and OCR work offline. Voice generation and conceptual search require optional model downloads; after downloading, inference runs on your device. Model availability, languages and download sizes are shown in the app. Voice and embedding models have their own licenses.

Piper Kareem is an optional Arabic voice. The rights for its training dataset are still unconfirmed pending clarification from upstream.

Papercut supports English and Arabic reading and audio workflows, alongside additional interface and voice languages. Android 8.0 or later and a 64-bit ARM device are required.

Report problems and request features at https://github.com/muhannadnouri/papercut.io/issues
